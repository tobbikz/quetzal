A

Epic Gantt — nested children with a hard “today” line

Hierarchy on the left, time on the right. The classic Jira / MS Project read: where are the epic bars, where is today, what slips.
Gantt · hierarchy
Problem vs currentCurrent view hides time. You see rows per sprint but not when work happens or how it stretches past the sprint boundary.
ProsInstantly familiar · epic+story rollup visible · scope creep obvious · sprint band + today line answer “are we on track”.
ConsNeeds real start/end dates (not just sprint). Can feel heavy for pure engineering teams used to boards.

Swimlane Timeline — one lane per teammate, 14 days across

Trade hierarchy for workload. Chips float on a per-person lane across a day axis — overlaps mean overload.
Swimlane · workload
Problem vs currentCurrent list says who owns what but not when their week is packed. Lanes make overload visually screamy.
ProsPerfect for standup / planning · instantly shows idle vs over-committed people · sprint boundaries are visible as dotted verticals.
ConsLoses parent/child epic rollup · a lot of chips if the team is small and chatty · needs date estimates per issue.

C

Now · Next · Later — three columns, each card gets a 12-week strip

Keep the discoverability of a column board but encode time inside every card. Every item knows where it lives in the next quarter.
Column board · time-aware
Problem vs currentCurrent sprint lanes answer “is it in a sprint?” but not “is it this month, next month, or later this quarter?”. This answers all three.
ProsLow ceremony · stays scan-friendly for PMs · each card’s strip is a mini-Gantt · column-level strip shows cross-item overlap in a quarter.
ConsNot for intra-sprint day planning · no explicit assignee lanes · dependencies are implicit, not drawn.

D

Portfolio Planner — initiatives as rows, quarters as columns

Zoom out past sprints. Each row is an initiative (not a ticket) stretched across one or more quarters, with a capacity strip across the top.
Portfolio · year view
Problem vs currentCurrent roadmap is sprint-local. Leadership and PMs can’t answer “what are we doing in Q2 ’27” from this screen today.
ProsPerfect for stakeholder reviews · surfaces capacity vs commitment per quarter · themes/owners obvious · dovetails with real OKRs.
ConsHides issue-level detail (needs drill-down) · requires initiatives to exist in the data model · not for daily use.

E

Dependency Timeline — arrows between blockers, capacity bars underneath

Make the critical path first-class. Draw dependencies as SVG arrows and stack a daily capacity meter under the chart so slippage becomes visible.
Gantt · dependencies · capacity
Problem vs currentCurrent roadmap doesn’t show that OPS-2 is blocked on OPS-3 or that OPS-4 is piling into a day where Marisol is already over.
ProsCritical path is readable at a glance · over-capacity days turn red · blockers highlighted on both axes · best for delivery managers.
ConsVisually denser than A/B · needs explicit blocked_by edges · arrows can clutter on big teams (toggle on/off).

Side-by-side. “Best for” is the primary audience; “Density” is how much the mock pushes per vertical pixel.
Concept Best for Shows time? Shows hierarchy? Shows workload? Shows deps? Density Risk vs current
A Epic GanttNested rows · today line · sprint bands Delivery lead reading an epic Yes · weekly Yes · parent/child Indirect Hint Medium Low — classic pattern users know
B Swimlane TimelinePer-assignee lanes, 14-day axis Standup / sprint planning Yes · daily No Yes — primary No Medium Low — maps closely to how teams actually plan
C Now · Next · Later + stripColumn board with per-card mini-Gantt PMs / stakeholder updates Yes · quarterly on each card Light No No Low Lowest — closest to the current mental model
D Portfolio PlannerInitiatives × 4 quarters + capacity top strip Leadership / FY planning Yes · quarterly Initiative only Capacity per quarter No Low Medium — requires an initiatives concept in the data model
E Deps + CapacityArrows between bars · daily capacity meter Delivery mgr / unblockers Yes · daily Flat Yes — daily meter Yes — primary High Highest — needs explicit blocked_by edges and point estimates

Recommendation shape

  • · Default view → A (Epic Gantt): smallest leap from current, biggest “oh, time” win.
  • · Secondary tab → B (Swimlane): directly replaces the sprint-lanes table with a time-aware equivalent.
  • · Zoom-out tab → D (Portfolio): once initiatives exist, this is the exec/FY view.
  • · Power-user toggle → E (Deps + capacity): enable on A/B as an overlay instead of a separate screen.
  • · C is the lightest ship but least differentiated — fine as a secondary mobile/compact layout.
